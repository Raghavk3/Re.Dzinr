The Project: IEEE INDICON is a premier annual international conference that serves as a global platform for researchers and academicians. To match the prestige of the event, we were commissioned to handle the end-to-end design and visual identity for the conference.
Our Impact: We transformed the conference's physical and digital presence by delivering a cohesive brand kit. Our work ensured that every touchpoint—from the moment a delegate received their ID card to the banners displayed on the main stage—reflected professional excellence.

-Deliverables: Full-scale printing kits, high-impact banners, delegate ID cards, academic posters, standees, and directional signage.

-Digital Reach: Designed a suite of digital creative media to drive online engagement and ensure a seamless aesthetic across social platforms.

-The Result: A visually unified event that facilitated a premium networking environment for international delegates and researchers.
