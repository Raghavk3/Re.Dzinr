The CMR Institute Of Technology Model United Nations (CMRITMUN) website was designed as the central digital hub for our conference. Serving as the landing page for all event-related information, the website was instrumental in handling the entire registration process for participants, ensuring a seamless and user-friendly experience. The site was delivered within the required deadlines and was crucial for the success of the event.

Built with Astro and utilizing modern front-end technologies, the website combines a dynamic design with smooth user interactions, providing an engaging experience for both participants and organizers. The design prioritizes functionality while maintaining an intuitive, clean interface that makes accessing essential conference information effortless.

Key project features:

User-friendly registration system with smooth sign-up flow

Real-time schedule updates and event details for attendees

Dynamic countdown timer for upcoming conference sessions

Mobile-responsive design for accessibility across devices

Admin panel for easy event management and content updates

Interactive conference map for venue navigation

The result is a polished and functional web platform that not only served the needs of the participants but also helped streamline the logistics of organizing a large-scale conference.
