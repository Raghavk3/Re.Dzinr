The Project: We partnered with Guru Nanak University to modernize their digital communication strategy. The goal was to translate complex academic structures into engaging, easy-to-digest digital content for prospective students and stakeholders.
Our Impact: Working closely with the university, we served as a creative consultant to bridge the gap between academic rigor and modern social media trends.

-Digital Course Elaboration: We redesigned the presentation of their course structures, making them visually accessible and mobile-friendly.

-Social Media Strategy: Crafted and drafted a series of social media campaigns that increased student interaction and brand affinity.

-Creative Storytelling: Moved away from traditional academic posting to a more "student-first" visual language.

-The Result: Improved digital literacy for the university’s offerings and a more vibrant, active social media community.
